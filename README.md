\# Retail Sales Data Exploration \& Analysis



\## Project Overview



This project analyzes retail sales data using Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib.



The project includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, business insights, visualizations, and report generation.



\## Dataset



The dataset contains retail sales information including:



\- Order Date

\- Region

\- State

\- Category

\- Sub-Category

\- Segment

\- Sales

\- Profit

\- Quantity



\## Data Cleaning



The dataset was checked for:



\- Missing values

\- Duplicate records

\- Data types



Two duplicate records were removed, resulting in 9,992 records.



\## Analysis Performed



\- Total Sales

\- Total Profit

\- Total Quantity

\- Profit Margin

\- Sales by Category

\- Profit by Category

\- Monthly Sales Trend

\- Top Sub-Categories by Sales

\- Sales by Region

\- Sales by Segment

\- Sales vs Profit relationship



\## Visualizations



The project includes:



\- Sales by Category

\- Monthly Sales Trend

\- Profit by Category

\- Top 10 Sub-Categories by Sales

\- Sales Distribution

\- Sales vs Profit
